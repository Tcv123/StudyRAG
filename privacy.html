Privacy

Your data, plainly put

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version. We store your account details and your revision progress so the site works. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not log your IP address. We never see your card number. You can delete your account, and everything tied to it, from Settings at any time.

1. Who we are

RAG Learning is operated by RAG Learning Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17472101), registered office 84 Wynchgate, London N14 6RN. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

For anything about your data, email TChandranivasan@raglearning.uk.

2. What we collect

Account details

  • Your first and last name, and email address.
  • Your password, which is stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We cannot see it.
  • Whether you registered as a student or a teacher, and your qualification level (GCSE or A-Level).
  • Your chosen avatar, which is a generated picture, not a photograph.

Your revision data

  • The subjects and exam boards you study, and any current or target grades you enter.
  • Your red / amber / green rating for each topic, including ratings set by diagnostics.
  • Practice and past-paper attempts: marks, percentages, number of questions answered and time taken.
  • Flashcards and decks you create, including any text you write on them.
  • Revision timetable preferences, such as the days and hours you are available to study, and any notes you add to a session.
  • Study timer totals and medals earned.

Subscription details

  • Which plan you are on, whether it is active, and when it renews or expires.
  • A customer reference issued by Stripe, our payment provider.
  • We never receive or store your card number. Card details are entered on Stripe's own payment pages and never pass through our systems.

Messages and feedback

  • If you contact us, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • If you answer the optional “how did you hear about us?” question, we store your answer and any comments you add. Answering is entirely optional.

Security records

If something looks like an attempt to change a protected part of an account, such as subscription status or administrator rights, we record the account involved, its email address, and what was attempted. This protects paying customers and keeps the site honest.

3. What we do not collect

  • No IP address logging in our own records.
  • No analytics or advertising. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no behavioural profiling and no selling of data to anyone, ever.
  • No tracking cookies. We do not set any cookies at all, which is why you will not see a cookie banner.
  • No date of birth and no identity documents.

4. Information stored in your browser

The site keeps a small amount of information in your browser's local storage, which is necessary for it to work. It stays on your device and is not sent to advertisers or any third party. It includes your sign-in session, your qualification level, whether your subscription is active, your study timer total, and any diagnostic or past-paper attempt you are part-way through, so you do not lose your answers if the page reloads. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

6. AI marking

When you ask for feedback on a written answer, the text of that answer, along with the question and the subject, is sent to Groq, which runs the AI model that produces the marking. Your name, email address and account identifier are not sent.

AI marking is there to help you practise. It is an estimate produced by software, not a mark awarded by an examiner, and it carries no weight with any exam board.

7. Teachers and classes

If you join a class using a code from your teacher, that teacher can see your name and your work for that class: your topic ratings, how many practice attempts you have made, your average and most recent scores, and when you were last active. They cannot see your email address, your password, your payment details or your work in other subjects.

Joining a class is always your choice, and you can leave a class at any time, which stops that teacher seeing your progress.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your account and revision data: for as long as your account exists.
  • If you delete your account: your profile, subjects, topic ratings, practice attempts, medals, flashcards, timetable and class memberships are deleted.
  • Security records: if a security event was recorded against your account, the record of what happened is kept, because its purpose is to document attempted misuse and deleting it would let someone erase their own trail. Your email address and account reference are removed from it when you delete your account, so it can no longer be linked to you.
  • Payment and sales records: kept for six years, as UK tax law requires. These are held by us and by Stripe.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You can act on most of these yourself in Settings: edit your details, change your email preferences, delete your diagnostic results, or delete your whole account. For anything else, email TChandranivasan@raglearning.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or by calling 0303 123 1113. We would rather you told us first, so we can put it right.

11. Age

RAG Learning is built for students taking GCSEs and A-Levels. You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian before buying a subscription, and the person who owns the card must agree to the payment.

If we learn that we hold an account for a child under 13, we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you by email.
