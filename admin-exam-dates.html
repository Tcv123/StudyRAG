Findings

api/cron/exam-timetable-check.js checks the four boards on the 1st of each month. A finding is a document it had not seen before — usually the next summer's timetable, sometimes an amendment to one you already approved. Parsed rows are guesses off a machine-read table; check each against the document before approving it.

Paste a timetable

The watch can only parse CSV. Board timetables are usually PDF or Excel, so this is the normal path: open the document, select the table, paste it here. Comma, tab or pipe separated all work, and a line without a date in it is skipped rather than guessed at.

What gets checked

The page on each board's site that the watch opens. These rot — boards reorganise — which is why they are editable here rather than constants in the function. If a board reports "could not reach the page", the watch is blind for it until the URL is fixed.
