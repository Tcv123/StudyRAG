🚦 Diagnostics

Test your knowledge on any topic below. Each diagnostic gives you a mix of questions and updates your RAG colour based on how you score — green means confident, amber means almost there, and red means it needs more revision.

👋 Getting started
Welcome to RAG Learning
Here's how to make the most of your revision — follow these steps and you'll know exactly what to focus on from day one.
🩺
Take a diagnostic
Open the Diagnostic tab and work through topics for each subject. It builds a personalised red / amber / green map of exactly what to prioritise — skipping it means revising blind.
Highly recommended first step
📊
Review your Breakdown
Head to the Breakdown page to see which topics are red or amber. These are the ones that need the most attention — focus your revision here first.
📝
Study with Notes & Practice
Use Notes to read up on weak topics and Practice Questions to test yourself. Both are available from day one — the diagnostic just tells you where to start.
🔁
Re-take diagnostics as you improve
Re-run diagnostics regularly to update your RAG colours. Watch your reds turn amber, and your ambers turn green. The goal: everything green before exam day.

We're always working to make RAG Learning better. All feedback is welcome — good or bad. If something isn't working, feels confusing, or you have an idea, we'd love to hear it.
